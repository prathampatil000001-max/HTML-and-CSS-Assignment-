CSS Image Filter Effects

Move your mouse over the images to see the effects.

1. Blur

filter: blur(5px);

2. Brightness

filter: brightness(150%);

3. Contrast

filter: contrast(200%);

4. Drop Shadow

filter: drop-shadow(5px 5px 10px black);

5. Grayscale

filter: grayscale(100%);

6. Hue Rotate

filter: hue-rotate(180deg);

7. Invert

filter: invert(100%);

8. Opacity

filter: opacity(30%);

9. Saturate

filter: saturate(200%);

10. Sepia

filter: sepia(100%);
